Component · FAQ

Native details. No JavaScript.

What is a tanking system, and do I need one?

Tanking is a waterproof membrane applied to walls and floors before tiling. In a wet room it is not optional. Tile and grout are not waterproof on their own. As approved Schlüter contractors we install it as a continuous layer, sealed at every junction and pipe penetration.

Do you work with architects and main contractors?

Yes. We are a pre-qualified accredited contractor and work to the drawing, the programme and the site's health and safety requirements.
