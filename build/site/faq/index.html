Questions we are asked most.

Tanking, materials, working with architects, and how we quote.

Tanking and wet rooms.

What is a tanking system, and do I need one?

Tanking is a waterproof membrane applied to walls and floors before tiling. Tile is impervious but grout is not, and neither is the substrate behind it. In a level-access wet room a tanking system is not optional; in any tiled shower it is strongly advisable. As approved Schlüter contractors we install it as a continuous layer, sealed at every junction and pipe penetration.

Is a waterproof board enough on its own?

No. Boards resist water, but the joints between them and every screw fixing through them are not sealed. The membrane bonds over the boards and bands every joint, which is what makes the surface genuinely continuous.

Can you form a level-access wet room on a timber floor?

Usually yes, depending on joist depth and whether the floor can be recessed to take the drain and falls. We check it on the survey because it affects both build-up and cost.

Choosing a surface.

Will marble stain in a bathroom?

Marble is porous and reactive. It can stain and etch from acidic products. Sealed properly and cleaned sensibly it performs well, and many people accept a degree of patina. If you want a surface that will not change at all, porcelain is the better answer and we will say so.

Quartz or granite?

Quartz is manufactured, so it is consistent and very low porosity. Granite is natural, with more variation and higher heat tolerance. Quartz for predictability, granite for character and durability.

Can quartz go outside or next to a hob?

Generally not. Resin-bound quartz can discolour under UV and can be damaged by direct heat. For outdoor or high-heat locations a sintered surface such as Dekton is the correct specification.

What is the largest tile you can install?

We regularly install 1200×2400 slabs and can handle larger. The constraint is usually access and substrate flatness rather than the tile.

Process and projects.

Do you work with architects and main contractors?

Yes. We are a pre-qualified accredited contractor, fully insured, and we work to the drawing and the programme. We raise setting-out queries early rather than resolving them silently on site.

Do you supply materials as well as install them?

We can supply and fit, or fit material you have sourced. Either way we review the specification before ordering. Some products are unsuitable for the substrate or the use intended.

How do you quote?

From a measured survey rather than a rate applied to an estimate. Send photographs, drawings or a schedule through the quote form and we will come back with questions or arrange a visit.

Which areas of London do you cover?

London and the surrounding area, with most of our work in Kensington and Chelsea, Westminster, Camden, Barnet, Wandsworth, Richmond upon Thames, and Hammersmith and Fulham.

Tell us about the space.

Send the rooms, the materials you are considering and your timeline. We will come back with a measured quote, not a guess from a price list.
