Every material behaves differently once it is on the wall.

Porcelain, natural stone, granite and engineered stone each need different substrates, adhesives and joint strategies. Choosing the material is the start of the specification, not the end.

Marble

Natural stone. Porous, reactive.Bathrooms, feature walls, low-traffic floors.

Limestone

Natural stone. Soft, absorbent.Hallways, living areas, honed floors.

Travertine

Natural stone. Naturally voided.Floors and walls, indoor and out.

Granite

Natural stone. Dense, hard-wearing.Entrances, lobbies, kitchen floors, external.

Quartz

Engineered. Consistent, low porosity.Worktops, vanities, internal surfaces.

Porcelain

Manufactured. Dense, durable.Almost anywhere, including wet areas.

What each material asks for.

MaterialPorosityBest suited toWatch for
MarblePorousBathrooms, feature walls, low-traffic floorsStains and etches; needs sealing and sensible cleaning
LimestoneVery porousHallways, living areas, honed floorsSoft and absorbent; sealer choice matters
TravertinePorous, voidedFloors and walls, indoor and outNatural voids may need filling; agree finish up front
GraniteLowEntrances, lobbies, kitchen floors, externalHard to cut; polished finishes can be slippery wet
QuartzVery lowWorktops, vanities, internal surfacesResin-bound, not for UV or direct heat
DektonNear zeroFloors, walls, worktops, externalSintered and rigid; unforgiving of a poor cut
PorcelainNear zeroAlmost anywhere, including wet areasLarge formats need a substrate within tolerance

From the quarry to the floor.

Natural stone is quarried, not manufactured. Blocks vary, slabs cut from the same block vary, and that variation is exactly why people specify stone in the first place.

Where a project calls for it we view and select the actual slabs, agree vein direction and sequencing before anything is cut, and dry-lay on site so the run can be seen before it becomes permanent.

Send the rooms, the materials you are considering and your timeline. We will come back with a measured quote, not a guess from a price list.
