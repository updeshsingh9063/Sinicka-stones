I

Tiling and stone, under one contractor.

Ceramic and porcelain, natural stone, granite, engineered stone, and fully tanked bathrooms and wet rooms, installed across London by NVQ Level 2 certified specialists.

Ceramic & Porcelain

Large-format porcelain and ceramic to walls and floors, including slabs that need dry-lay, vacuum handling and tight substrate tolerance.

Natural Stone

Marble, limestone, travertine and onyx, specified, sourced, sequenced, set out and sealed.

Granite

Dense, hard-wearing stone for high-traffic floors, cladding, stair treads and thresholds.

Engineered Stone

Quartz, Dekton and Silestone: manufactured surfaces with predictable performance.

Bathrooms & Wet Rooms

Fully tanked wet rooms using the approved Schlüter system, installed before any tile goes down.
