Third Space, Wimbledon.

Large-format porcelain, terrazzo and glazed ceramic across changing rooms, locker bays and circulation in a health club that runs wet all day.

Watch the project film
Location
Wimbledon Quarter, London
Sector
Commercial leisure
Main contractor
Woodford Interiors Ltd
Scope
Floor and wall tiling, changing rooms, circulation

Materials

Four finishes, one set of tolerances.

The specification moves between four families and holds a single standard of setting out across all of them.

Large-format porcelain runs through the changing rooms and circulation, laid to falls where it needs to drain and flat where it does not.

Terrazzo picks up the borders and thresholds. Glazed green ceramic, stacked in some runs and stepped in others, carries the wet walls. Brass-toned tiling wraps the reception desk and a curved wall at the entrance, where every cut is visible at eye level.

The difficult part is not any single finish.

It is that the joints have to line through as you move between them.
