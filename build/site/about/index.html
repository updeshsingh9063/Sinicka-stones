A London contractor, not a showroom.

Kroll Interiors delivers wall and floor tiling and stone work for private clients, developers and main contractors across London and the UK.

Tiling and stone, for the construction industry.

We specialise in high-quality wall and floor tiling and stone work for the construction industry, for private and commercial clients across London and the UK.

We work alongside architects, designers and main contractors, and directly with private clients. On commercial projects that means being pre-qualified, insured and able to work to the drawing and the programme. On private work it means turning up when we said we would and explaining what we are doing and why.

Our clients include Kings Cross, Mizen Group, Brennen Group, Calma Group and WeLink Homes.
