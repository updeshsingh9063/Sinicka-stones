What you need from a tiling subcontractor.

On a commercial fit-out the finish is only half the question.

The other half is whether the subcontractor is pre-qualified, properly insured, safe on site, and able to hold a programme when the trade before them has overrun.

We are a pre-qualified accredited contractor with fully insured, NVQ Level 2 certified installers. We work from the drawing, raise setting-out queries before they become variations, and coordinate with the engineer and main contractor where the build-up touches structure, services or waterproofing design.

Where waterproofing is in scope, the warranty is being relied on.

We install the Schlüter tanking system as approved contractors.
