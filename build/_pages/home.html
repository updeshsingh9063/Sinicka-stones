Tile and grout are not waterproof.

Tile is impervious. Grout is not, and neither is the substrate behind it. Water finds the joints, then the fixings, then the timber or plasterboard underneath.

What keeps it out

A tanking membrane, bonded to the walls and floor before tiling and sealed at every corner, penetration and drain upstand.

How we tank a wet room

The build-up

A finish is only as good as what sits behind it. The part you see is the last layer.

Substrate, tanking and movement joints are specified before a single tile is cut. It is the part nobody sees, and the only reason the part they do see still looks right in ten years.

Our approach
  1. 05Tile and groutLarge format, back-buttered to full coverage. The only layer anyone sees.
  2. 04Adhesive bedNotched and beaten in. Voids under a tile are where cracks start.
  3. 03Schlüter tanking membraneOne continuous layer, banded at every corner, joint and penetration.
  4. 02Backer boardUncoupling where the structure is expected to move.
  5. 01SubstrateSurveyed, levelled and laid to falls before anything else.
