Working with architects and engineers

We build to the drawing.

On specified projects we work from the architect or designer’s drawings and raise setting-out queries early, rather than resolving them silently on site.

Tile module against room dimensions, where cuts land, how thresholds meet adjoining finishes, and where movement joints have to fall are all questions worth answering before anything is fixed.

Where the build-up interacts with structure, services or waterproofing design, we coordinate with the engineer and the main contractor. Substrate deflection, floor levels and drainage falls are not tiling decisions alone.

Fewer surprises, fewer variations.

And a finish that matches what was drawn.

From the quarry to the floor.

Natural stone is quarried, not manufactured. Blocks vary, slabs cut from the same block vary, and that variation is exactly why people specify stone in the first place.

Where a project calls for it we view and select the actual slabs, agree vein direction and sequencing before anything is cut, and dry-lay on site so the run can be seen before it becomes permanent. Batching, lead times and shade consistency are confirmed at order rather than discovered at delivery.

For engineered stone and porcelain the questions are different: format, finish, slip resistance and whether the product is rated for where it is going.
