Every material behaves differently once it is on the wall.

Porcelain, natural stone, granite and engineered stone each need different substrates, adhesives and joint strategies. Choosing the material is the start of the specification, not the end.

From the quarry to the floor.

Natural stone is quarried, not manufactured. Blocks vary, slabs cut from the same block vary, and that variation is exactly why people specify stone in the first place.

Where a project calls for it we view and select the actual slabs, agree vein direction and sequencing before anything is cut, and dry-lay on site so the run can be seen before it becomes permanent.
